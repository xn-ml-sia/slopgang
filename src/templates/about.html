Five principlesFive principles

Not rules. These are the questions we ask about each thing we build.

  1. 01Quiet until neededA thing should be calm when nothing happens, and clear when something does.
  2. 02One thing firstShow the one thing people came for. The rest can wait.
  3. 03Less on the screenRemove what does not help. Then look again.
  4. 04Motion means something movedAnimate a change, not decoration. With motion off, you lose nothing.
  5. 05Made for people we knowWe build for friends and family. That keeps each app small and personal.
