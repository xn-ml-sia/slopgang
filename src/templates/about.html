I make complicated things feel calm.I make complicated things feel calm.

I’m Noa Lindgren, a product and interaction designer in Copenhagen. I grew up in Malmö, studied interaction design at the Royal Danish Academy, and spent five years designing newsroom and archive tools before going independent in 2019.

I work as the only designer alongside a client’s engineers, usually for six to eighteen months: long enough to ship, measure and fix. I like problems where a mistake costs someone something real: money at a till, a train, a night’s sleep.

Curriculum vitae

Five principlesFive principles

Not rules: the questions I keep asking until a design answers them.

  1. 01Quiet until neededAn interface should be calm when nothing is happening and exact when something is.
  2. 02One number firstFind the thing people came for and put it where their eyes land. Everything else can wait a step.
  3. 03Measure the real thingStopwatches on platforms, counts from the log, a phone held one-handed on a bus.
  4. 04Motion means something movedAnimate a change of state, never decoration. With motion off, nothing should be lost.
  5. 05Leave it better documentedEvery project ends with the system written down, so the next person does not need me.
